# Role assessment

Use the role description, verification result, current private brief, CV and professional experience. Assess each distinct vacancy selected for substantive review, including those subsequently excluded.

1. Identify what the hire would own. Compare substantive duties and required experience with specific documented projects, transferable skills and technical foundations. Preserve the scale and setting of the candidate's contribution. Employer openness to learning can make personal projects or demonstrated AI interest relevant.
2. Explain the fit in words and distinguish evidence from judgement. Evaluate geography, practical eligibility, full-time paid work, compensation, workload, travel and contract duration. Unknowns and preferences deserve explicit treatment. Treat confirmed closure as a reason to exclude from application recommendations while retaining the fit judgement. Use a hard exclusion for an evidenced conflict with an essential condition or clearly unsuitable duties. If context is missing, label the assessment provisional and withhold personalised claims.
3. Decide recommendation disposition independently of intake and fit. Recommend suitable roles with a concrete application path. Use follow-up for promising unresolved intake, eligibility or material user decisions, including internal-only holds. Record why any matching role was held, evidence, who can resolve it, the action and next check date. Include every assessed suitable distinct role in the relevant report section.
4. Rank application priority using this run's verified intake, essential eligibility, evidence of relevant experience, urgency and practical trade-offs. Identify a concrete action and one supportable CV angle for each top candidate. A strong fit with an unresolved material condition can have deferred priority.

Return the fit rationale, constraints, disposition, priority rationale and next action per vacancy. Aim for 15 new opportunities as an indicative volume. Include all assessed suitable roles and promising follow-ups, with no score cutoff or ceiling. The original rubric specified weights of 35/20/20/15/10. Use qualitative judgement until the ordered criteria, rating scales and calculation rules are supplied.
