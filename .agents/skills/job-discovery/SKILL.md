# Job discovery

Start with the current brief, the UK and European early-career startup market, and recent reports if available. Use broad role-family and responsibility queries together. Search founder's office, founding operator, operations associate and graduate, business, strategy, commercial, revenue, product and customer operations, implementation, deployment, technical support operations, AI operations and automation. Investigate adjacent titles when the work fits.

Look for ownership of ambiguous projects, working with founders, customer troubleshooting, closing product feedback loops, improving systems and implementing automations. A meaningful subset is enough to investigate. Historical calibration examples are DASH Rides — Founders Associate, Lucid Computing — Operations Associate, Novabook — Startup Operations Graduate, Nevoya AI — Operations Associate, Kinship — Junior Operations Specialist, Nous — Operations & Automation Associate and Medelic — Founder's Associate. These names describe past examples. Establish current intake independently.

1. Search direct startup careers pages and employer ATS boards, Y Combinator Work at a Startup, Wellfound, Welcome to the Jungle and Startup.jobs. Cover Seedcamp, Index Ventures, Balderton and relevant VC or accelerator portfolios. For stale or gated marketplace listings, trace the employer’s careers index to a role-specific route. Where historical denominators are reliable, use actionable yield and unique contributions to adjust effort. Reserve exploration for other sources. Exclude LinkedIn, including indexed LinkedIn results.
2. Follow promising results to the vacancy. Capture company, title, location if known, source and listing URL where first found, secondary sightings and candidate application route. A later employer verification does not change discovery attribution.
3. If a portfolio board is unreadable, select a reasonable sample of relevant portfolio companies, inspect direct careers pages and log which companies were attempted, reached and productive. Retain the portfolio referral and actual discovery channel. Record limitations or a failed fallback.
4. Return candidate leads and a coverage ledger by source, query or company, including access outcome and scope. Distinguish indexed snippets, actual visits, potential vacancies and duplicates. Pass this coverage to reporting for reconciliation with actual assessments and actionable opportunities. Leave unmeasured volumes unknown. Bound coverage claims to the pages and queries actually inspected.
